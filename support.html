FAQ

Distance or route is incomplete

Go to Settings > PunKunYai > Location, choose "While Using the App" or "Always", and turn on "Precise Location". You can lock the screen during a ride and recording continues, but don't swipe the app away in the app switcher.

I forgot to finish a trip

Open the app again and it will ask whether to finish or discard the unfinished trip.

Where are my photos and videos?

In your Photos library, and on the trip's summary screen in the app.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
